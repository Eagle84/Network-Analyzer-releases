Why Network 3D Live

Know your network like the back of your hand

A fast, visual way to see what's connected, how strong it is, and whether anything doesn't belong.

📡

Instant device discovery

Scan your Wi-Fi and see every connected device in seconds — with IP, vendor, and hostname where available.

🗺️

Live 3D map

Watch your network come alive as an interactive map of nodes, so you can see how everything connects at a glance.

🛡️

Spot intruders

New or unknown devices are flagged, so you'll notice when something joins your network that shouldn't be there.

📶

Signal & reach

Check signal strength and connection quality to find dead spots and the best place for your router.

⚡

Router quick access

Jump straight to common router admin pages (including TP-Link) to manage your network without hunting for the address.

🔒

Private by design

Scans run locally on your phone. Your network details stay with you — nothing is uploaded to a server.

Getting started

From install to full map in seconds

1
